About Timeline by Harsh

Timeline by Harsh is a Hindi-first Instagram page by Harsh Upadhyay. It explains Hindu history, temples, traditions, festivals and scriptures through simple storytelling. The audience is mainly in India.

Who I work with

  • Rudraksha and devotional product brands
  • Puja samagri makers
  • Book publishers
  • Hindu education platforms
  • Faith-tech and puja-booking platforms
  • Temple and pilgrimage service providers

Ways to work together

  • Dedicated Instagram Reels
  • Educational content around a product
  • Instagram Stories
  • Festival campaigns
  • Longer partnerships
  • Affiliate and performance-based collaborations

How I work

  • I do not promise that any puja, mantra, gemstone or product will guarantee wealth, marriage, success or protection.
  • I verify product details before I make claims about Rudraksha, gemstones or spiritual products.
  • Sponsored and affiliate content is clearly labelled, for example Ad, Sponsored, Collaboration or Affiliate.
  • I keep historical evidence, religious belief, scriptural interpretation and regional practice separate.
  • For services such as puja booking, I verify the provider, the ritual, the price and the confirmation first.
