Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Short version: this site does not set its own cookies and has no analytics or advertising trackers. A few services it uses may set theirs.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. Sites use them to remember things such as a login or a payment in progress.

What this site does

This website does not set cookies of its own, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It does not store information in your browser to follow you around.

What other services may do

Some parts of the page load from other companies. When they load, they may set their own cookies or similar storage, which I do not control:

  • Cal.com: the booking calendar on the page.
  • Razorpay: its checkout script loads with the page, and its secure checkout opens when you pay. It may use cookies or similar storage to prevent fraud and complete your payment.
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts. Your browser contacts Google to fetch them.
  • Amazon: only if you click a book link and leave this site.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block everything, parts of the booking calendar or checkout may stop working.

More information

Read the privacy policy to see how your information is used. Message @timelinebyharsh on Instagram, or reply to your booking confirmation email or WhatsApp message.
