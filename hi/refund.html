Refunds and Rescheduling

Aakhri update: 8 November 2026

Chhote mein: call se kam se kam 6 ghante pehle bata dein toh time free mein badal sakte hain. Agar main aapki call nahi kar paaya, toh poora refund milega.

Rescheduling

Call se kam se kam 6 ghante pehle WhatsApp par ya confirmation email ka reply karke bata dein, hum bina extra kharche ke doosra time tay kar lenge.

When you get a full refund

  • Main call cancel kar doon ya aa na paaoon aur hum naya time tay na kar paayein.
  • Meri taraf ki kisi technical dikkat ki wajah se call na ho paaye.
  • Ek hi booking ke liye aapse ek se zyada baar paise kat jaayein, ya payment ho gaya par booking confirm nahi hui.

When a refund is not given

  • Aap call par nahi aaye aur pehle bataya bhi nahi.
  • Aapne call se 6 ghante se kam pehle cancel karne ko kaha. Aise mein bhi jahan ho saka main ek baar time badalne ka mauka doonga.
  • Call ho chuki hai. Salah wapas nahi ki ja sakti, isliye poore ho chuke session ka refund nahi hota.

Agar kuch gadbad hui aur upar ki koi baat fit nahi baithti, toh mujhe likhein. Main imaandaari se dekhkar case ke hisaab se faisla karoonga.

How refunds are paid

Manzoor hua refund Razorpay ke zariye usi payment tareeke mein wapas jaata hai. Isme aam taur par 5 se 7 kaam-kaaji din lagte hain, aapke bank ya UPI app ke hisaab se.

How to ask

Instagram par @timelinebyharsh ko message karein, ya apni booking confirmation email ya WhatsApp ka reply karein. Kripya woh naam aur email batayein jisse book kiya tha, aur payment ID agar aapke paas ho.

Ye niyam terms of use ke saath chalte hain. Inse Indian consumer law mein aapka koi haq kam nahi hota.
