Namaste, main Harsh hoon

Main Harsh Upadhyay hoon, Timeline by Harsh (@timelinebyharsh) ke peeche. Main Instagram par Hindu dharmik content banata hoon aur Sanatan Dharma aur roz ki zindagi par logon se baat karta hoon.

Log aksar ek jaisi dikkatein lekar aate hain: zyada sochna, aisa routine jo tikta nahi, ya koi bada faisla jo akele lena mushkil hai. Iske liye ek comment ya chhota message kaafi nahi hota, isiliye main one-on-one calls karta hoon.

Call kya hai

Google Meet par ek private baatcheet, bas aap aur main. Aap batate hain ki kahan phanse hain. Main seedhe sawaal poochkar asli dikkat dhoondta hoon. Phir hum aane wale dinon ke liye kuch saaf kadam tay karte hain. Baad mein 24 ghante ke andar main WhatsApp par likhit summary bhejta hoon.

Main samajhdari ki baaton, roz ki practice jaise dhyan, aur Sanatan Dharma se seekhi baaton se kaam leta hoon. Main bas motivation wali baatein nahi karta.

Call kya nahi hai

  • Ye therapy, counselling ya medical salah nahi hai.
  • Ye legal, paison ya investment ki salah nahi hai.
  • Ye kisi nateeje ki guarantee nahi hai. Mehnat aapko karni hai, main bas saaf dekhne mein madad karta hoon.

Agar aap mushkil mein hain ya safe mehsoos nahi kar rahe, toh apni local emergency service (India mein 112) ya kisi bharosemand insaan se turant sampark karein.

Aapki privacy

Aap jo batate hain woh sirf hamare beech rehta hai. Main sirf wahi jaankari leta hoon jo booking aur taiyaari ke liye zaroori hai. Poori jaankari privacy policy mein hai.

Baat karne ke liye taiyaar?

Aap apne liye sahi session chun sakte hain ya seedha time chun sakte hain. Pehle kuch poochna ho toh Instagram par @timelinebyharsh ko message karein, ya apni booking confirmation email ya WhatsApp ka reply karein.
