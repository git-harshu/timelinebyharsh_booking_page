Why I take calls

Log aksar ek jaisi dikkatein lekar aate hain: zyada sochna, aisa routine jo tikta nahi, ya koi bada faisla jo akele lena mushkil hai. Iske liye ek comment ya chhota message kaafi nahi hota, isiliye main one-on-one calls karta hoon.

How a call works

01

You talk

Aap batate hain ki kahan phanse hain. Aaram se bolein, koi jaldi nahi hai.

02

I ask

Main seedhe sawaal poochta hoon taaki asli dikkat milein, sirf sabse zor wali nahi.

03

We plan

Hum aane wale dinon ke liye kuch saaf kadam tay karte hain. 24 ghante ke andar main WhatsApp par likhit summary bhejta hoon.

What I draw on

Samajhdari ki baatein, roz ki practice jaise dhyan, aur Sanatan Dharma se seekhi baatein. Bas motivation wali baatein nahi.
