The Hindu Festival and Vrat Companion

Aapke tyohaaron aur vraton ke liye ek guide: har ek ka matlab, taiyaari kaise karein, aur kya-kya chahiye.

Who it is for

  • Jo log tyohaar aur vrat rakhte hain aur sirf "kaise" nahi, "kyun" bhi jaanna chahte hain.
  • Parivaar jo ghar par ek saaf reference chahte hain.
  • Jo abhi shuru kar rahe hain aur samajh nahi paa rahe ki kahan se shuru karein.

What is inside

  • Major festivals explained
  • Preparation checklists
  • Common puja samagri lists
  • Basic ritual steps
  • Mantra meanings and pronunciation
  • Traditional variations and references
  • Common questions answered

How it is made

Riwaaj kshetra, calendar aur parampara ke hisaab se alag hote hain. Jahan farq hai, guide use saaf likhti hai, ek tareeke ko hi akela tareeka nahi batati. Sources ka naam diya jaata hai. Ye kisi puja, mantra ya product se kisi nateeje ka vaada nahi karti.

Price and format

Free Hindu Festival and Vrat Calendar pehle aayega. Poori guide paid download hogi, aur uski keemat pay karne se pehle dikhayi jaayegi.

Common questions

When will it be ready?

Main abhi ise bana raha hoon. List join karein, tayyar hote hi main aapko likhunga.

Is it free?

Calendar free hai. Poori guide paid download hogi, aur keemat pay karne se pehle dikhayi jaayegi.

How will I get the free calendar?

Early-access list join karein. Calendar tayyar hote hi main use aapko email kar doonga.

What format will the guide be in?

Ek download karne wali guide jo aap phone par padh sakte hain ya print kar sakte hain. Bikri se pehle main exact format bata doonga.

Which festival dates will it follow?

Tyohaaron ki tithiyan har saal badalti hain aur calendar aur kshetra ke hisaab se alag hoti hain. Main likhunga ki har tithi kaun sa calendar follow karti hai, aur aap apne local panchang ya pandit se confirm zaroor karein.

Will it match my family's tradition?

Ye likhti hai ki kshetra aur parampara ke hisaab se riwaaj kahan alag hain, aur sources ka naam deti hai. Ye har parivaar ki parampara ko cover nahi kar sakti, isliye apne parivaar ya pandit se zaroor poochein.

Do you perform pujas?

Nahi. Puja preparation session sirf planning aur seekhne ke liye hai. Puja ke liye kisi qualified pandit ke saath kaam karein.

Can I suggest a topic?

Haan. Instagram par message karke bata dein ki kaun sa tyohaar ya vrat aap chahte hain.
