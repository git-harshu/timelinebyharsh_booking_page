Privacy Policy

Aakhri update: 8 November 2026

Is page mein bataya gaya hai ki website use karne ya call book karne par kaun si personal jaankari li jaati hai, kyun li jaati hai, aur aapke paas kya haq hain.

Who is responsible

Timeline by Harsh, jise Harsh Upadhyay ("main") chalate hain, yahan batayi gayi personal jaankari ke liye zimmedaar hai. Instagram par @timelinebyharsh ko message karein, ya apni booking confirmation email ya WhatsApp ka reply karein.

What I collect

Jab aap priority slot book karte hain, form mein ye poocha jaata hai:

  • Aapka naam, email aur WhatsApp number
  • Aapki umar aur city
  • Aapne kaun sa session chuna, aur pasand ki date aur time
  • Aap kis baare mein baat karna chahte hain, aur koi aur baat jo aap batana chahein
  • Agar aap UPI se payment karte hain, toh payment ka reference number (UTR) aur woh email jisse aapne book kiya
  • Agar aap early-access list join karte hain, toh aapka email, optional WhatsApp number aur woh topics jo aap chunte hain

Calendar se book karne par scheduling service woh jaankari maangti hai jo use chahiye, jaise aapka naam aur email.

Aapki dikkat ka byora personal ho sakta hai. Kripya wahi batayein jisme aap comfortable hon.

Main aapke card, UPI ya bank ki detail nahi dekhta aur na rakhta hoon. Payment Razorpay sambhalta hai.

Why I use it

  • Aapki call tay karne aur Google Meet link bhejne ke liye
  • Call ki taiyaari karne aur WhatsApp par likhit summary bhejne ke liye
  • Payment confirm karne aur booking emails bhejne ke liye
  • Aapne jo guides, calendar aur updates maange hain woh bhejne ke liye
  • Aapke sawaalon ke jawaab dene, time badalne ya refund karne ke liye, aur woh records rakhne ke liye jo kanoon maangta hai

Main aapki jaankari bechta nahi hoon aur advertising ke liye use nahi karta.

Who else handles it

Service chalane ke liye main in companies ki madad leta hoon. Sabki apni privacy policy hai.

  • Razorpay: payment sambhalta hai. Aapki booking ki jaankari payment record ke saath save hoti hai.
  • Cal.com aur Google (Calendar, Meet aur Gmail): scheduling, video call aur booking emails.
  • WhatsApp: follow-up messages aur aapki likhit summary.
  • Google Fonts: is site ke fonts load karta hai, yaani aapka browser Google se judta hai.
  • Amazon: kitaabon ke links affiliate links hain. Link par click karne ke baad ki baatein Amazon ki site par Amazon sambhalta hai.

Agar kanoon maange toh main jaankari share kar sakta hoon.

How long I keep it

Booking ki jaankari main tab tak rakhta hoon jab tak service dene, aapse follow-up karne aur tax-accounting ke niyam nibhane ke liye zaroori hai. Uske baad jo hata sakta hoon, hata deta hoon. Razorpay ke paas rakhe payment records uske apne niyamon se chalte hain.

Your choices and rights

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ke tehat aap mujhse pooch sakte hain ki mere paas aapki kaun si jaankari hai, use theek karwa sakte hain ya hatwa sakte hain, aur apni sahmati wapas le sakte hain. Iske liye upar bataye tareeke se sampark karein. Main jald se jald jawaab doonga. Santusht na hon toh aap Data Protection Board of India mein shikayat bhi kar sakte hain.

Children

Agar aapki umar 18 saal se kam hai, toh kripya mata-pita ya guardian ki jaankari aur sahmati se hi book karein. Main umar isiliye poochta hoon.

Security

Ye site HTTPS par chalti hai aur payments Razorpay ke secure checkout se hote hain. Koi bhi system poori tarah surakshit nahi hota, isliye kripya booking form mein sensitive documents ya passwords na bhejein.

Cookies

Cookie policy dekhein.

Changes

Agar main ye policy badloonga toh upar ki date badal doonga. Bade badlaav hone par home page par bata doonga.
