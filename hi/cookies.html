Cookie Policy

Aakhri update: 8 November 2026

Chhote mein: ye site apni koi cookie nahi lagati aur isme koi analytics ya advertising tracker nahi hai. Isme use hone wali kuch services apni cookies laga sakti hain.

What a cookie is

Cookie ek chhoti file hoti hai jo website aapke browser mein save karti hai. Sites inse login ya chal rahe payment jaisi baatein yaad rakhti hain.

What this site does

Is website ki apni koi cookie nahi hai, aur ye analytics, advertising ya tracking tools use nahi karti. Ye aapke browser mein aisi koi jaankari save nahi karti jisse aapka peecha kiya ja sake.

What other services may do

Page ke kuch hisse doosri companies se load hote hain. Load hone par woh apni cookies ya waisi hi storage laga sakti hain, jin par mera control nahi hai:

  • Cal.com: page par booking ka calendar.
  • Razorpay: iski checkout script page ke saath load hoti hai, aur pay karte samay secure checkout khulta hai. Ye fraud rokne aur payment poora karne ke liye cookies ya waisi storage use kar sakta hai.
  • Google Fonts: fonts load karta hai. Iske liye aapka browser Google se judta hai.
  • Amazon: sirf tab jab aap kitaab ka link kholkar is site se bahar jaayein.

Your choices

Aap apne browser ki settings mein cookies block ya delete kar sakte hain. Sab kuch block karne par booking calendar ya checkout ka kuch hissa kaam karna band kar sakta hai.

More information

Aapki jaankari kaise use hoti hai, ye jaanne ke liye privacy policy padhein. Instagram par @timelinebyharsh ko message karein, ya apni booking confirmation email ya WhatsApp ka reply karein.
