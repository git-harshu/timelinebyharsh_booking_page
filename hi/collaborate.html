Un brands, publishers aur platforms ke liye jo Hindu itihaas, mandir, rituals aur shaastra mein dilchaspi rakhne wali audience tak pahunchna chahte hain.

Timeline by Harsh ke baare mein

Timeline by Harsh, Harsh Upadhyay ka Hindi-first Instagram page hai. Ye Hindu itihaas, mandir, paramparayein, tyohaar aur shaastra ko aasaan kahaniyon mein samjhata hai. Audience zyada tar India mein hai.

Main kinke saath kaam karta hoon

  • Rudraksha aur devotional product brands
  • Puja samagri banane wale
  • Kitaab publishers
  • Hindu education platforms
  • Faith-tech aur puja-booking platforms
  • Mandir aur teerth yatra service providers

Saath kaam karne ke tareeke

  • Dedicated Instagram Reels
  • Kisi product ke aas-paas educational content
  • Instagram Stories
  • Tyohaar campaigns
  • Lambi partnerships
  • Affiliate aur performance-based collaborations

Main kaise kaam karta hoon

  • Main kisi puja, mantra, ratna ya product se dhan, vivah, safalta ya suraksha ki guarantee ka vaada nahi karta.
  • Rudraksha, ratna ya spiritual product ke baare mein kuch kehne se pehle main specifications check karta hoon.
  • Sponsored aur affiliate content par saaf label hota hai, jaise Ad, Sponsored, Collaboration ya Affiliate.
  • Itihaas ke saboot, dharmik maanyata, shaastra ki vyakhya aur kshetriya riwaaj main alag-alag rakhta hoon.
  • Puja booking jaisi services ke liye main provider, ritual, keemat aur confirmation pehle verify karta hoon.

Sampark karein

Instagram par @timelinebyharsh ko message karein, aur batayein: aapka brand, product ya service, aap kis audience tak pahunchna chahte hain, aur aapki timeline. Reach aur audience ki details main maangne par share karta hoon.
