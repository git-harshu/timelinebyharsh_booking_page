Service

Timeline by Harsh mein Harsh Upadhyay ke saath Google Meet par private one-on-one guidance call milti hai. Abhi ye sessions hain: Clarity Call (30 minute), Dincharya Reset (45 minute) aur Deep Dive (60 minute). Keemat Indian rupees mein booking section aur checkout mein dikhti hai.

Ye service kya nahi hai

Ye calls samajhdari ki baaton aur Sanatan Dharma ki parampara par aadharit guidance wali baatcheet hain. Ye therapy, counselling, medical, legal, paison ya investment ki salah nahi hain, aur main kisi licensed professional ki jagah nahi le sakta. Aise maamlon mein kripya sahi professional se milein.

Main kisi khaas nateeje ka vaada nahi karta. Call ke baad aap kya karte hain, ye aapka faisla hai.

Ye emergency service nahi hai. Agar aap mushkil mein hain ya safe mehsoos nahi kar rahe, toh apni local emergency service (India mein 112) se sampark karein.

Booking aur payment

  • Aap calendar se book kar sakte hain ya priority slot ke liye payment kar sakte hain. Payment Razorpay sambhalta hai.
  • Aapka slot tab pakka hota hai jab aapki booking ya payment confirm ho jaaye.
  • Calendar se ki gayi booking tab tak sirf request hoti hai jab tak main use confirm na karoon. Agar aap UPI se pay karte hain, toh payment dikhne ke baad main booking confirm karta hoon.
  • Kripya sahi jaankari dein. Mujhe aapse email ya WhatsApp par sampark karna pad sakta hai.
  • Time badalne aur refund ke niyam refund policy mein hain.

Kaun book kar sakta hai

Aapki umar 18 saal ya usse zyada honi chahiye. Usse chhote hon toh kripya mata-pita ya guardian ki sahmati se hi book karein.
