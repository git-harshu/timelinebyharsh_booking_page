Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This page explains what personal information is collected when you use this website or book a call, why, and what your choices are. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

Who is responsible

Timeline by Harsh, run by Harsh Upadhyay ("I", "me"), is responsible for the personal information described here. Message @timelinebyharsh on Instagram, or reply to your booking confirmation email or WhatsApp message.

What I collect

When you book a priority slot, the form asks for:

  • your name, email address and WhatsApp number
  • your age and city
  • the session you choose, and your preferred date and time
  • a short description of what you want to talk about, and any extra details you choose to share
  • if you pay by UPI, the payment reference (UTR) and the email you booked with
  • if you join the early-access list, your email, an optional WhatsApp number and the topics you pick

When you book through the calendar, the scheduling service asks for the details it needs, such as your name and email.

Your description of your problem can be personal. Please share only what you are comfortable with.

I do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled by Razorpay.

Why I use it

  • to schedule and hold your call, and send the Google Meet link
  • to prepare for your call and send you a written summary on WhatsApp
  • to confirm your payment and send booking emails
  • to send you the guides, calendar and updates you asked for
  • to answer questions, handle rescheduling or refunds, and keep records the law requires

I do not sell your information, and I do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

To run the service I rely on these providers. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Razorpay: processes payments. Your booking details are saved with the payment record.
  • Cal.com and Google (Calendar, Meet and Gmail): scheduling, video calls and booking emails.
  • WhatsApp: follow-up messages and your written summary.
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts on this site, which means your browser contacts Google.
  • Amazon: the book links are affiliate links. If you click one, Amazon handles what happens on its site.

I may also share information if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep booking details for as long as needed to deliver the service, follow up with you, and meet tax and accounting rules. After that I delete what I can. Payment records held by Razorpay follow its retention rules.

Your choices and rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask me to show you the information I hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent. To do any of these, contact me as above. I will reply as soon as I can. If you are not satisfied, you can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

If you are under 18, please book only with a parent or guardian's knowledge and consent. I ask for your age so I can check this.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and payments use Razorpay's secure checkout. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive documents or passwords through the booking form.

Cookies

See the cookie policy.

Changes

If I change this policy I will update the date at the top. For major changes I will say so on the home page.
