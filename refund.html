Refunds and Rescheduling

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Short version: you can move your call for free if you tell me at least 6 hours before. If I cannot hold your call, you get a full refund.

Rescheduling

Tell me at least 6 hours before your call, on WhatsApp or by replying to your confirmation email, and we will pick another time at no extra cost.

When you get a full refund

  • I cancel the call or cannot attend and we cannot agree a new time.
  • The call cannot happen because of a technical problem on my side.
  • You were charged more than once for the same booking, or the payment went through but the booking was not confirmed.

When a refund is not given

  • You do not join the call and did not tell me in advance.
  • You ask to cancel less than 6 hours before the call. In this case I will still offer one reschedule where I can.
  • The call has taken place. Advice cannot be returned, so completed sessions are not refunded.

If something went wrong and none of these fit, please write to me. I will look at it fairly and decide case by case.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. This usually takes 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank or UPI app.

How to ask

Message @timelinebyharsh on Instagram, or reply to your booking confirmation email or WhatsApp message. Please include the name and email you booked with, and the payment ID if you have it.

These rules sit alongside the terms of use. They do not remove any right you have under Indian consumer law.
