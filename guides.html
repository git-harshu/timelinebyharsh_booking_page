The Hindu Festival and Vrat Companion

One guide for the festivals and vrats you observe: what each one means, how to prepare, and what you will need.

Who it is for

  • People who observe festivals and vrats and want to know the why, not only the how.
  • Families who want one clear reference at home.
  • Anyone starting out who is not sure where to begin.

What is inside

  • Major festivals explained
  • Preparation checklists
  • Common puja samagri lists
  • Basic ritual steps
  • Mantra meanings and pronunciation
  • Traditional variations and references
  • Common questions answered

How it is made

Practices differ by region, calendar and tradition. Where they do, the guide marks the variations instead of presenting one way as the only way. Sources are named. It does not promise any result from a puja, mantra or product.

Price and format

The free Hindu Festival and Vrat Calendar comes first. The full guide will be a paid download, and its price will be shown before you pay.
