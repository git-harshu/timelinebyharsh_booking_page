Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and preparing for your 1:1 call.

How and where does the 1:1 call happen?
All calls happen privately over Google Meet. When you book via Cal.com or the priority checkout, a secure Google Meet link is automatically created and sent to your email and WhatsApp number.
Why is there a ₹299 commitment fee?
Earlier, Harsh offered calls for free. Unfortunately, many people booked slots and never showed up, blocking others who genuinely needed help. The ₹299 commitment fee ensures only serious, committed individuals attend so Harsh can dedicate 100% of his energy and preparation to you.
Can I reschedule if something urgent comes up?
Yes. Life happens. You can reschedule your session by notifying at least 6 hours in advance via WhatsApp or reply to your confirmation email, and we will pick a new slot at no extra charge.
Is this conversation completely private and confidential?
100% yes. Whatever you discuss with Harsh — whether personal life decisions, career choices, or struggles — remains strictly between you and Harsh. We never share or publish client session details.
How should I prepare before joining the call?
Keep a pen and notepad ready. Write down your #1 question and what you have already tried so far. Be ready to be open and honest — the more context you provide, the faster Harsh can help you solve it.
