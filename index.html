Common questions

Cannot find your answer? Message me and I will reply.

Message on Instagram
Where does the call happen?

Privately over Google Meet. After you book, a calendar invite with your Meet link reaches your email and WhatsApp.

Which session should I pick?

Start with the Clarity Call. If you need more time after that, we can plan a longer session.

Why do the calls have a fee?

When calls were free, many people booked and did not show up, which blocked others who needed the time. A small fee keeps slots for people who will attend, and lets me prepare properly for you.

Can I reschedule if something comes up?

Yes. Tell me at least 6 hours before, on WhatsApp or by replying to your confirmation email, and we will pick another time at no extra cost. More in the refund policy.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. What you share stays between you and me. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is this therapy or medical advice?

No. These are guidance conversations, not therapy, medical, legal or financial advice. Read the terms.

How should I prepare?

Keep a pen and paper nearby. Write down your main problem and what you have already tried, so we can start at the root.
