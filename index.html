Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about the call format, preparation, and scheduling.

How does scheduling through Cal.com work?
Cal.com connects directly with Harsh's Google Calendar. When you pick a date and time on the calendar, your slot is instantly reserved and a Google Meet invitation is sent directly to your email inbox. There is zero waiting or manual back-and-forth needed.
Can I discuss both Track A and Track B in one 30-minute call?
In 30 minutes, doing both tracks properly is difficult. It is recommended to choose the single track that is most urgent right now. If you need in-depth guidance on both social media growth and spiritual sadhana, you can book two separate slots.
Why is there a ₹299 fee?
When calls were free, people booked slots and never showed up. The ₹299 is a small commitment filter so that only serious, committed individuals book, allowing us to make full, focused use of every minute.
What if I need to reschedule?
For Cal.com bookings, you can reschedule in one click directly from your Google Calendar invitation email. For priority bookings, send a message on WhatsApp at least 6 hours before the call and we will move your slot for free.
Is our conversation confidential?
Yes, 100%. Everything discussed during your call is strictly confidential. Harsh never shares client names, personal situations, or conversation details publicly.
What happens after the call finishes?
Within 24 hours of the call, Harsh will send a short summary note directly to your WhatsApp detailing what was agreed and your top 3 prioritized action items.
