If you don't know your Kuldevta or Kuldevi, what should you do?

Begin by returning towards your roots — ask the elders, grandparents, or village pandit of your native place. This search is itself a form of kulsadhana, because when we seek our roots, we connect with our inner self. If the name is still not found, light a diya with a calm mind and pray to Kaalbhairav or Maa Kali — they are the eternal witnesses beyond time, observing every lineage and soul since the very beginning. When the Kuldevta is unknown, they are revered in their place.

After marriage, whose Kuldevta should be worshipped — maternal or husband's lineage?

Viewed deeply, marriage is not merely the union of two bodies, but the meeting of two lineages and their guardian deities. When a woman honors both traditions, she becomes a sacred bridge between both energy streams — and from that balance flows stability, peace, and auspiciousness in the home. There is no need for rigid conflict; wherever the heart bows with sincere devotion, divine blessing flows naturally.

Yes, 100%. Everything discussed during your call is strictly confidential. Harsh never shares client names, personal family matters, or conversation details publicly.

Within 24 hours of the call, Harsh will send a short summary note directly to your WhatsApp detailing what was agreed and your top 3 prioritized action items.
