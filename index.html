Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where and how does the call take place?

Every session happens privately over Google Meet. When you book via Cal.com or the priority checkout, a calendar invitation with your secure Meet link is automatically sent to your email and WhatsApp.

Why is there a ₹299 commitment fee?

When calls were free, many booked slots without showing up, blocking people who genuinely needed guidance. A nominal ₹299 fee filters for committed individuals and ensures Harsh can dedicate uninterrupted time and preparation to you.

Can I reschedule if an emergency comes up?

Yes. If you notify at least 6 hours in advance via WhatsApp or by replying to your confirmation email, we will pick another time slot at no extra charge.

Is the conversation kept confidential?

Strictly confidential. Whatever personal, professional, or creative struggles you share remain solely between you and Harsh.

How should I prepare for the call?

Keep a pen and paper beside you. Write down your main problem and what you've already tried so we can get straight to the root without wasting the first 10 minutes.
