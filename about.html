About

Hi, I am Harsh

I am Harsh Upadhyay, the person behind Timeline by Harsh. I create Hindu religious content on Instagram and talk with people about Sanatan Dharma and everyday life.

Book a call@timelinebyharsh on Instagram

Why I take calls

The problems people bring are usually familiar: too much overthinking, a routine that keeps slipping, or a big decision that is hard to make alone. A comment or a short message is rarely enough for that, which is why I offer one-on-one calls.

How a call works

01

You talk

You explain where you are stuck. Take your time. Nobody is rushing you.

02

I ask

I ask direct questions to find the real problem, not just the loudest one.

03

We plan

We agree a few clear steps for the days ahead. I send a written summary on WhatsApp within 24 hours.

What I draw on

Practical wisdom, daily practices such as dhyan, and what I have learned from Sanatan Dharma. Not generic motivation.

What a call is not

Not therapy

It is not therapy, counselling or medical advice.

Not professional advice

It is not legal, financial or investment advice.

Not a guarantee

You do the work. I help you see it clearly.

If you are in crisis or feel unsafe, please contact your local emergency services (in India, call 112) or someone you trust.

Your privacy

What you tell me stays between us. I collect only what is needed to book and prepare for your call.
