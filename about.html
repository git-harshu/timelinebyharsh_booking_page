About

Who I am and how these calls work

Hello, I am Harsh

I am Harsh Upadhyay, the person behind Timeline by Harsh (@timelinebyharsh). I create Hindu religious content on Instagram and talk with people about Sanatan Dharma and everyday life.

The problems people bring are usually familiar: too much overthinking, a routine that keeps slipping, or a big decision that is hard to make alone. A comment or a short message is rarely enough for that, which is why I offer one-on-one calls.

What a call is

A private conversation over Google Meet, just you and me. You explain where you are stuck. I ask direct questions to find the real problem. Then we agree on a few clear steps for the days ahead. Afterwards I send you a written summary on WhatsApp within 24 hours.

I draw on practical wisdom, daily practices such as dhyan, and the values I have learned from Sanatan Dharma. I will not give you generic motivation.

What a call is not

  • It is not therapy, counselling, or medical advice.
  • It is not legal, financial, or investment advice.
  • It is not a guarantee of any result. You do the work, I help you see it clearly.

If you are in crisis or feel unsafe, please contact your local emergency services (in India, call 112) or a trusted person nearby.

How I look after your privacy

What you tell me stays between us. I collect only what is needed to book and prepare for your call. You can read the details in the privacy policy.

Ready to talk?

You can find which session fits or go straight to choosing a time. If you have a question first, message @timelinebyharsh on Instagram, or reply to your booking confirmation email or WhatsApp message.
